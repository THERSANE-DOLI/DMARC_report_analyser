How does it work?

  • Mailbox providers (Google, Microsoft, Yahoo…) send a compressed report every day to the rua= address of your DMARC record.
  • Drop one or more of these files above: they are read directly in your browser and are not sent to any server.
  • A message passes DMARC when DKIM or SPF is valid and aligned with the From: domain.
